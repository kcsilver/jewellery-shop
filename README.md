## Images

The gallery uses local image files in `assets/`. The showroom photo was supplied by the shop, and the style-inspiration photos are from Pexels under its free-use license. The stock photos are illustrative only and do not represent Padma Jewellers products or endorsements.

## Metal rates

The page displays IBJA domestic benchmark rates through the [Dharun India API](https://api.dharun.dev/v1/bullion). Gold rates are per 10 grams; silver is converted from the published per-kilogram rate. Rates exclude GST and making charges, and local retail prices may vary.
